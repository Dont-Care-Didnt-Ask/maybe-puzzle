What counts as success?

Puzzles: follow the published Lichess solution, including all your moves in the line. The opponent replies automatically. Equivalent alternatives are not accepted in this MVP.

Ordinary positions: play a legal move no more than 0.5 pawns below Stockfish's best precomputed evaluation. Every legal move is graded offline; each position has at least two accepted moves. This engine-based filter is approximate; “ordinary” does not mean “no tactics.”

Your first attempt determines the score. You can retry a mistake, but credit is not restored. Skipping, revealing the answer, and finishing a started-but-unfinished attempt count as unsuccessful. Untouched positions are not scored.

Ordinary positions currently come from a random sample of high-rated Lichess games (both players at least 1800; the default source is an even stronger Elite subset).

This is a static, unrated self-training tool. The mixture and solutions are hidden in the interface, not secured against inspection in browser developer tools. Sessions exist only in this tab's memory.
